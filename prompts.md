🎯 Prompt 1 — Homepage Copy

🛠 Tool Used: Claude AI

📌 Goal:
Generate homepage copy including a headline, tagline, value proposition, Why Choose Us section, and welcome message.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💬 Prompt

You are a professional website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

Write homepage copy for a salon and beauty parlour called "Glowup Studio" located in Hyderabad, Telangana.

The copy must include:

1. A strong headline (maximum 10 words)

2. A catchy tagline (maximum 15 words)

3. A value proposition paragraph (3–4 sentences, warm and persuasive tone)

4. A "Why Choose Us" section with 4 bullet points

5. A short welcome message (2–3 sentences)

Target Audience:
Women aged 18–45

Tone:
Warm, confident, welcoming, and professional.

Make the content website-ready and conversion-focused.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ Why This Prompt Works

• Clearly defines the business name and location.

• Specifies the exact website sections required.

• Defines the target audience for more relevant content.

• Establishes a clear brand tone and voice.

• Includes structure and output requirements.

• Uses word limits to keep the content concise and website-friendly.

• Encourages persuasive and conversion-focused copy rather than generic AI-generated text.
